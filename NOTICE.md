# Credits and scope

Diamant was designed by **Alan R. Moon and Bruno Faidutti**. The referenced tabletop editions are published by IELLO and, in Russian, Hobby World. Published tabletop illustrations are credited to Paul Mafayon.

This repository is an **unofficial fan adaptation** implementing three documented base-game/relic rules profiles. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the designers or publishers. It does not include the Betrayal/Warnings expansions or every game sold under the Incan Gold name.

No publisher card scans, original logo or rulebook pages are shipped in the application. All raster illustrations under `public/art/` were generated specifically for this project. Prompt provenance is recorded in `docs/art-direction.md`. The website title is text, not a reproduction of the publisher's logo.

Official rule PDFs are development references downloaded from the publishers; they are ignored by Git and are not served publicly. `references/sources.json` records their provenance and SHA-256 hashes. `docs/research/rules-audit.md` is an independently worded mechanics specification.

Application dependencies retain their own licenses. Lucide icons are distributed under the ISC License; React is distributed under the MIT License.
