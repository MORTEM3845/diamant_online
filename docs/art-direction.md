# Art direction and asset provenance

All nine raster illustrations were created for this project on 2026-11-13 using the built-in OpenAI ImageGen tool. These are original generated compositions; no publisher illustrations, cards, booklets, logos, or box artwork were used as source images or tracing references. The game mechanics and the names of its subjects informed the brief.

## Visual direction

A tactile gouache and woodcut adventure world: midnight teal and emerald cavern shadows, warm amber lantern light, ruby coral treasure, turquoise mineral accents. Environmental images leave a quiet central or left area for real HTML interface text. Card images have bold readable silhouettes, with no numbers, borders, or text baked into the illustrations.

## Production

- Environments: 1536 × 1024 pixels.
- Card art: 720 × 720 pixels.
- Format: WebP, quality 84, method 6, RGB.
- Postprocessing: conversion and proportional downsampling only, with Pillow/Lanczos; no compositing or image edits.
- Original generated PNG files are retained in the generation output, outside the Git repository. The WebP files below are the deployment assets.

## Assets and complete prompts

### public/art/cave-entrance.webp

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: wide landscape background for an elegant browser board game about exploring caves for gemstones. Primary request: an original cinematic hand-painted expedition illustration, landscape 3:2, dense mysterious jungle around an ancient cavern entrance in a massive cliff, one tiny explorer with warm amber lantern walking toward the entrance, jewel-like turquoise glimmers emerging from its deep darkness. Composition: the cave entrance and the detailed picturesque landscape are on the right half; the left third is very dark quiet jungle shadow and atmospheric mist, useful negative space for cream typography that will be added in HTML. Style: sophisticated illustrated board game, painterly gouache, cut-paper edge rhythm, woodcut ink shadow detail, premium storybook art, tactile matte paper grain; NOT photorealism or 3D. Color palette: deep blue-green, ink navy, muted emerald, warm copper and golden lamplight; hints of dusky blue mist. Lighting: dusk, luminous mysterious contrast. No lettering, logos, numbers, UI, borders, watermark, publisher characters or recognizable existing composition. Rich finished illustration, evocative adventure but calm refined visual.
